# Clear Path Content Guide

`AGENTS.md` remains authoritative. This guide governs non-public social/content deployment assets.

## Voice

Write like a credible Alexandria-area owner-operator: local, direct, conversational, and specific about what the photo proves. Prefer short sentences and ordinary language. Avoid corporate filler, exaggerated claims, fake urgency, emoji clutter, hashtag walls, and repetitive sales language.

Approved factual location language is **Alexandria, Louisiana**, **Alexandria, LA**, or **Central Louisiana** when supported. Use **property cleanout**, **junk removal**, **household debris**, **cleared space**, and **photo estimate** naturally. Do not label a customer a landlord, investor, tenant, estate representative, or property manager without approved evidence.

## Authentic images and before/after claims

- Originals are immutable and stay in `source/`.
- Never use generative editing to add/remove debris, repair damage, alter the property, or make a result more dramatic.
- Modest crop, resize, straightening, exposure/contrast correction, compression, conversion, privacy-safe redaction, and restrained brand treatment are allowed.
- A before/after pair requires shared architecture: windows, doors, cabinets, fixtures, walls, flooring, room shape, and compatible viewpoint.
- If the evidence is uncertain, describe two scenes separately. Never imply a same-room transformation.
- Branding stays secondary. The photograph must remain useful if the logo or business name is removed.

## Privacy

Inspect each source and each processed export for mail, names, addresses, phone numbers, documents, license plates, faces, people, reflections, and other identifying details. Exclude the image or redact only the sensitive region. Record the action in `privacy-review.md`. Do not disclose the exact street address, customer identity, private messages, or customer pricing.

## Calls to action

Preferred CTAs:

- Text photos for a quote.
- Send us photos for an estimate.
- Need a property cleared out?
- Request an estimate.
- See the full cleanout.

Use `318-290-8863` only when a phone number helps the platform and the caption remains readable. No fake scarcity, discounts, deadlines, load counts, crew claims, turnaround claims, or price claims.

## Link selection

Choose one existing page based on intent:

- Visual proof or complete transformation: existing project/case-study page.
- High service intent: relevant service page.
- Local Alexandria discovery: Alexandria service-area page.
- Process or cost education: relevant existing article when the caption matches it.
- General brand post: homepage.

Confirm the destination returns successfully before marking an item ready. Do not create a website page merely to give a campaign a destination.

## Platform differences

- **Facebook:** conversational proof, a clear local/service takeaway, light CTA, 0–3 hashtags.
- **Instagram:** strong first line, visual emphasis, concise body, local reference, restrained hashtags.
- **Nextdoor:** neighborhood-relevant observation or useful insight; least promotional; CTA sparingly.
- **Google Business Profile:** completed work, service, location, direct next step; no keyword stuffing or unverified offer.
- **Pinterest:** traffic-oriented title, natural search language, concise description, accurate alt text, and the most relevant existing URL.

## Intent balance

Classify every manifest item as `conversion`, `trust`, `traffic`, or `engagement`. Across a useful campaign, aim roughly for 35% conversion, 30% trust/proof, 25% traffic, and 10% engagement, but follow what the photographs actually support. Platform variants of one concept do not count as distinct content opportunities.

## Naming and optimization

Use lowercase kebab-case with place, scene, state, and platform when useful, for example `alexandria-kitchen-cleanout-before-after-instagram.webp`. Never stretch a photo. Prefer WebP/JPEG, sensible dimensions for the platform, and compression that preserves visible detail. Keep source provenance even when processed filenames change.

## Repetition rule

One job should normally yield 5–8 genuinely different concepts, not a wall of near-identical ads. Distinct concepts may include hero transformation, property-owner relevance framed generally, larger-cleanout capability, local proof, photo-estimate education, exterior cleanup, a room-specific transformation, or case-study traffic. Each concept needs its own objective.
