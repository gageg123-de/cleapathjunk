Common questions

Appliance Removal FAQs

Do appliances need to be disconnected?

Yes, appliances should generally be safely disconnected, emptied, and ready for removal unless a different scope is confirmed in advance.

Can you remove a refrigerator from upstairs?

Access must be reviewed first. Send photos of the refrigerator, stairs, landings, and doorways.

Can appliances be picked up with other junk?

Yes. Combining appliances with furniture or household debris can be included in one photo estimate.

What does pricing depend on?

Type, weight, access, labor, volume, and disposal requirements all affect final pricing.
