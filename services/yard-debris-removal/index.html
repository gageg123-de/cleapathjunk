Common questions

Yard Debris Removal FAQs

Do you remove all landscaping waste?

Service depends on material, volume, and disposal requirements. Text photos before scheduling so the pile can be reviewed.

Can you take mixed outdoor junk?

Appropriate non-hazardous outdoor clutter may be included. Separate and identify any chemicals, fuel, paint, batteries, or unknown containers.

Should branches be bundled?

Not always, but a clear, consolidated pile makes estimating and loading more predictable.

Can exterior cleanup be part of a property cleanout?

Yes. Appropriate exterior debris can be included in an agreed full-property scope.
