Common questions

Garage Cleanouts FAQs

Do I need to organize everything first?

You do not need a perfect garage, but items to keep should be clearly separated from the removal pile.

Can you remove paint or chemicals?

Do not place hazardous or unknown materials in the removal pile. Send photos and identify questionable items before scheduling.

Can the garage be included with the house?

Yes. Garage contents can be scoped with a full property or estate cleanout.

How do I get an estimate?

Text wide photos of the garage plus close photos of especially heavy or unusual items, along with your city and access details.
