Common questions

Furniture Removal FAQs

What does a single-item pickup start at?

Published single-item pricing starts at $75. Final pricing depends on the item, access, labor, and disposal requirements.

Can you remove furniture from upstairs?

Potentially. Send photos and describe the stairs, hallways, and any tight turns so the access can be assessed safely.

Do you take mattresses?

Mattresses and box springs are common pickup items, subject to final disposal and access details.

Can furniture be part of a full cleanout?

Yes. Furniture removal is commonly combined with property, estate, rental, and garage cleanouts.
