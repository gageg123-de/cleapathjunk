Common questions

Estate Cleanouts FAQs

Can the cleanout happen in stages?

Yes, depending on the project. A clear staged scope can help when family members are still sorting important belongings.

Will you decide what to donate or keep?

No. Customers should identify keepsakes, documents, valuables, and retained items before the agreed removal begins.

Do you remove hazardous materials?

No hazardous-waste service is advertised. Flag chemicals, paint, fuel, medical waste, and unknown materials before scheduling.

Can you also clear the garage?

Yes, ordinary non-hazardous garage contents can be included after the scope and access are reviewed.
